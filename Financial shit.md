## TMM

- **TMM** stands for **Taux du Marché Monétaire** (_Money Market Rate_).
- It is the official interest rate at which **Tunisian commercial banks lend money to each other** for short periods, calculated and published by the Central Bank of Tunisia (BCT).
### Why It Matters to You

The TMM is the **nationwide benchmark** that dictates almost every bank rate in Tunisia:
https://mybank.tn/ar/guide/tmm-tunisie.html

1. **For Borrowers (Loans):** Bank loans (car, home, business) are usually priced as `TMM + Bank Margin` (e.g., 7%+3%=10% interest). When TMM goes up, your monthly loan payments increase.
    
2. **For Savers (Term Deposits / DAT):** Banks use TMM as the ceiling and yardstick for how much interest they are willing to pay you to lock up your cash. If they can borrow from other banks at ~7.00% (the TMM), they won't pay you significantly more than that for a deposit.
    
In short: **TMM is the base price of money in Tunisia.**
