



- Unlimited Namespacing: You can use as many colon-separated levels as you need. For example, `app:v1:auth:tokens:refresh:${accountId}:${tokenId}` is totally a valid key
- - **Namespace Convention:** The colon `:` is simply a community convention (like path separators `/` in URLs) used to group related keys together visually and logically.
